How to request deletion

  1. From the same account you used to contact us, send "Please delete my data" to our Facebook Page on Messenger or to our Instagram account by Direct message. You can also contact us on LINE (@infinite5000) or by phone (+886 2 2952 5859).
  2. After confirming the request comes from you, we delete the conversation records, draft replies and attachments related to you from our customer service system within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Messages stored on Meta's platforms (Facebook / Instagram) are handled under Meta's policies; you can also delete the conversation yourself in Messenger or Instagram. If the law requires us to keep certain records (for example purchase or warranty records), we will tell you what is kept and why.

See our Privacy Policy for details.
