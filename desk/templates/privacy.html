適用範圍

本政策說明無限科技（以下稱「我們」）透過 Facebook 粉絲專頁 Messenger、Instagram 私訊與留言回覆客人時，如何蒐集與使用您的資料。我們的客服工作台只用於回覆您主動傳給我們的訊息。

我們收到的資料

  • 您傳給我們粉絲專頁或 Instagram 帳號的訊息內容（文字、圖片、影片、時間），以及您在我們貼文下的公開留言。
  • Meta 提供的您的公開名稱或 Instagram 使用者名稱，以及 Meta 為這段對話指定的編號。
  • 如果您是點廣告進來，Meta 提供的廣告標題。

我們不會向 Meta 索取您的好友名單、個人檔案其他內容或任何與這段對話無關的資料。

使用目的

只用來回答您的詢問：產品、報價、安裝預約、售後服務。每一則回覆都由我們的人員確認後才送出。我們不把您的資料用於廣告投放，也不出售或出租給任何人。

AI 擬稿

為了更快回覆，我們的系統會使用 OpenAI API 先擬一份回覆草稿給客服人員參考。送出前，系統會遮蔽電話、車牌、Email、身分證字號、LINE ID 與已知格式的姓名地址，也不會傳送您的名稱或 Meta 編號；請求設定為不保存（store=false）。草稿一律由人員審核、修改後才會送給您。

資料提供對象

僅限提供服務所需：Meta（訊息平台本身）、Render（我們的主機與資料庫）、OpenAI（擬稿，內容已遮蔽如上）。除法律要求外，不會提供給其他第三方。

保存與刪除

對話紀錄保存在我們的資料庫，只在提供客服與保固售後需要的期間保留。我們隨訊息寄給您的圖片、影片附件，在我們系統中最多保存 30 天。您可以隨時要求刪除，方法見 資料刪除說明。

您的權利

依台灣《個人資料保護法》，您可以查詢、閱覽、要求補正、停止使用或刪除您的資料。

Scope

This policy explains how WirelessLife (無限科技影音有限公司, "we") collects and uses data when we reply to customers through our Facebook Page (Messenger), Instagram Direct messages and comments. Our customer service desk is used only to answer messages you send to us.

Data we receive

  • The messages you send to our Facebook Page or Instagram account (text, photos, videos, time), and public comments you leave on our posts.
  • Your public name or Instagram username provided by Meta, and the ID Meta assigns to the conversation.
  • If you came from an ad, the ad title provided by Meta.

We do not request your friends list, other profile information, or any data unrelated to the conversation.

How we use it

Only to answer your inquiry: products, quotes, installation appointments and after-sales service. Every reply is reviewed and approved by a staff member before it is sent. We do not use your data for advertising, and we never sell or rent it.

AI-assisted drafting

To reply faster, our system uses the OpenAI API to prepare a draft reply for our staff. Before a request is made, phone numbers, license plates, email addresses, ID numbers, LINE IDs and recognizable names and addresses are masked, and your name and Meta IDs are not sent; requests are made with storage disabled (store=false). A person reviews and edits every draft before anything is sent to you.

Who receives data

Only the providers needed to run the service: Meta (the messaging platform itself), Render (our hosting and database) and OpenAI (drafting, masked as described). We do not share data with other third parties unless required by law.

Retention and deletion

Conversation records are kept in our database only as long as needed for customer service and warranty support. Photos and videos we attach to replies are kept in our system for at most 30 days. You can ask us to delete your data at any time; see the data deletion instructions.

Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act you may ask to access, correct, stop using or delete your data.
